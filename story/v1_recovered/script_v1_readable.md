## CAST

- **centurion** — Titus Arrius (Centurion, Legio X (player)): PLACEHOLDER NAME, not canonical. Fictional. Newly promoted in 58 BCE, about 28. Dry, quick, proud, wants Caesar's notice. Speaks less as the campaign goes on; by Act III he speaks in Caesar's cadence. Ends alive, at Uxellodunum, looking at his hands.
- **caesar** — Gaius Julius Caesar (Proconsul, commander in Gaul) [historical]: Historical. Warm, funny, exact, brave, never raises his voice to be obeyed. Explains atrocity with clarity and composure. No personality switch: the man in ch12 is the man in ch1. All lines are placeholder invention unless tagged as a paraphrase of BG.
- **sextus** — Sextus Naevius (Optio of the centurion's century; his closest friend): PLACEHOLDER NAME, not canonical. Teases the centurion relentlessly in Act I. Never a moralist. Comes to fear his friend's composure, not his rage. The tell is that he stops calling him Titus.
- **fufius** — Gnaeus Fufius (Tesserarius; later lent to the quaestor as tally clerk): PLACEHOLDER NAME, not canonical. Good with numbers, fussy, kind to mules. Counting captives at the Aduatuci upsets him; by Alesia he runs the distribution, and at Uxellodunum he keeps the tally. Becomes cruel through routine, not appetite.
- **bassus** — Bassus (Legionary; gambler and joker): PLACEHOLDER NAME, not canonical. Runs a book on everything. Never stops being cheerful, and that becomes one of the most disturbing things in the game.
- **opimius** — Opimius (Signifer of the century): PLACEHOLDER NAME, not canonical. Sings on the march in Act I. Gavius's tent-mate. Speaks less and less after Britain; in Act IV he says almost nothing and does what he is told.
- **gavius** — Publius Gavius (Veteran legionary; writes letters for men who cannot): PLACEHOLDER NAME, not canonical. In his forties. The older comrade. Mentors the recruit Tiro. Dies in the surf off Britain, 55 BCE. His letter formula ('The food is bad. The centurion is fair.') outlives him.
- **tiro** — Decimus, called Tiro (Recruit, joins 55 BCE): PLACEHOLDER NAME, not canonical. Ploughboy from the Po valley. Worships the centurion as the centurion worships Caesar. Hears the argument at Avaricum. Dies at Gergovia carrying the centurion's message.
- **ritumara** — Ritumara (Aeduan interpreter): PLACEHOLDER NAME, not canonical. Fictional Aeduan noblewoman attached to the grain contracts. Has a brother in Aeduan politics and a son, Tascos. Ally in 58, leaves when the Aedui break with Rome in 52, captured with the relief army and set aside at Alesia as one of the useful Aedui, translates again in 51 because her son is a hostage.
- **labienus** — Titus Labienus (Legate, Caesar's second) [historical]: Historical. Dry, professional, the soldier's officer. Never sentimental. Presence in a given scene is tagged.
- **aquilifer** — Aquilifer of the Tenth (Eagle-bearer, unnamed in the source) [historical]: Historical but unnamed (BG 4.25). One paraphrased line. Do not give him a name.
- **vercingetorix** — Vercingetorix (Arvernian war leader) [historical]: Historical. Seen, not heard: he surrenders at Alesia in silence. No lines on purpose.
- **tribune** — Aulus (tribune) (Young military tribune of the Tenth): PLACEHOLDER NAME, not canonical. One of the young men from the city who panicked at Vesontio (BG 1.39). Delivers the Tenth's thanks the next morning (BG 1.41). Sends the centurion as a runner at the Sabis.
- **recruit** — Marcus (recruit) (Replacement recruit, 51 BCE): PLACEHOLDER NAME, not canonical. Stands where Tiro stood. Receives the argument in Act IV.
- **legionary** — Legionary (Generic soldier of another century or legion): Generic voice for one-off barks.
- **mercator** — Trader (Roman slave dealer following the army): PLACEHOLDER NAME, not canonical. Polite, brisk, honest by his own lights. BG 2.33 says the buyers reported the count.

TRIBUNE: Centurion. You're not, ah, sealing anything? (young, red-eyed, trying for casual)
CENTURION: Nothing to leave, tribune. I spent it.
FUFIUS: Centurion. Runner from the praetorium. The general wants you. (out of breath, impressed)
SEXTUS: Wash your face. He'll think we keep you in a ditch.
